Methodology

Overall is a composite, not a verdict.

Benchsy weights six practical capabilities. A specialist can be the right choice even when it ranks lower Overall.

How to read the scores Prices, context windows, release dates and status come from provider documentation. The 0–100 capability scores are Benchsy's own normalization across the tracked set — the scale leaves headroom above today's leaders, so the strongest current result sits at 98 rather than a perfect 100 — and each one carries its benchmark basis on hover in the fingerprint panel.
